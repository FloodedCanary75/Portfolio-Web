How it works

Verlet integration

The candy stores its current and previous position; velocity is just the difference. No explicit integration error, and it stays stable at any frame rate.

Rigid cord solving

Each cord is solved fourteen times per frame against the shared candy position, so the cords stay dead straight while still allowing slack when you pull.

Pointer slicing

Every pointer move becomes a segment, tested against each live cord. A hit converts the cord into a hanging eight-node stub that swings on its own.
